DixSecura Privacy Policy

Effective from 16 November 2026 · Applies to the DixSecura app for Android and iPhone and the DixSecura visitor web pages

In short

  • DixSecura is a workplace security app. It is needed to bring a phone into Dixon secured sites, and it keeps the phone's camera from being used while the phone is inside one.
  • It uses your phone's location to know when you are inside a secured site, including when the app is closed.
  • It never takes, views, copies or uploads your photos, videos, messages, contacts or files.
  • It has no advertising and no analytics, and your information is not sold.
  • Visitor records are deleted 90 days after the visit. You can ask for your information to be removed sooner.

1. Who is responsible

DixSecura is operated by Dixon Technologies (India) Limited ("Dixon", "we"). Dixon decides how the information described here is used and is responsible for it. Contact details are in section 12.

This policy covers three kinds of people: Dixon employees who register a phone, visitors to Dixon sites, and Dixon staff who approve visitors or work at the security gate.

3. What we do not collect

  • Photos, videos, screenshots or audio from your phone.
  • Contacts, call history, SMS or other messages, email or calendar.
  • Files, browsing history, or what you type.
  • What you do inside other apps.
  • Advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

4. Phone permissions

Android

  • Location, "Allow all the time". Used as described above, including in the background, so protection starts when you enter a site even if the app is closed.
  • Accessibility service. DixSecura uses Android's Accessibility service for two purposes only: to notice when a camera screen opens while the phone is inside a secured site and close it, and to stop DixSecura from being removed while the phone is registered. The service does not read, store or send what is shown on your screen, what you type, your messages or your passwords.
  • Usage access (optional). Used only to identify which app opened the camera inside a site, so the correct app is closed and named in the alert.
  • Display over other apps (optional). Used to show a "camera not allowed here" notice.
  • Notifications. To show the protection status and, for approvers, that a visitor is waiting.
  • Run at start-up and unrestricted battery use. So protection keeps working after a restart and is not stopped by the phone's battery saver.

iPhone

  • Location, "Always". Used as described above.
  • Screen Time. With your permission, the apps you choose (Camera and other apps that can take pictures) are locked while the phone is inside a secured site. Your choice of apps and any Screen Time information stay on your phone. Dixon receives only whether the lock is ready and active.
  • Notifications. As for Android.

Removing the app

While a phone is registered, DixSecura prevents its own removal. To remove it, ask Dixon Security for a one-time release code. Once the code is accepted, all restrictions are lifted and the app can be uninstalled.

5. How the information is used

  • To protect confidential information at Dixon sites by keeping phone cameras from being used there.
  • To issue and check gate passes, and to keep the visitor and entry registers.
  • To let the person you are visiting approve your entry.
  • To investigate security alerts.

We use the information because you agreed to it when you registered the phone or requested a visit, and because Dixon needs it to keep its sites secure and meet its legal obligations. It is not used for advertising or marketing.

6. Who can see it

  • Dixon Security and authorised administrators can see registered phones, alerts, gate records and visitor records.
  • The Dixon employee you are visiting can see your visit details and the status of the visits they host.
  • Authorised IT staff who maintain the DixSecura server.

The information is stored on servers operated by Dixon. We do not sell it and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires it.

Your phone's own location service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) works out the phone's position. Their handling of that is covered by Google's and Apple's privacy policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information. Ask us what we hold about you and to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Have it deleted. Ask us to delete your information before the periods above. We will do so unless we must keep it for an open investigation or by law.
  • Withdraw your consent. You can stop using DixSecura at any time: ask Dixon Security for a release code, then uninstall the app. Without the app, the phone cannot be taken into a secured site.
  • Switch off permissions. You can change the permissions in your phone's settings. Inside a secured site this is reported to Security as an alert, and the gate pass will not work until protection is restored.
  • Complain. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, contact us first. You may also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

To use any of these, write to the contact in section 12. Please include your name and, for a visit, the visit date or reference, so we can find your record. We aim to reply within 30 days.

9. How it is protected

  • The app talks to the server only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Each phone signs its messages with a key kept in the phone's secure hardware, so another device cannot pretend to be it.
  • Passwords are stored only in a protected (hashed) form.
  • Access to the records is limited by role and requires a personal sign-in; actions by staff are logged.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

DixSecura is a workplace app for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how your information is used in an important way, Dixon Security will also inform registered users before it applies.
